### Resume adopts an orphaned harbor job (added 20:58, 41/41 overall)
Design change: `kill -9` of the pipeline kills only Python. The harbor child keeps running
the trials, and their conversations live in its memory. Resume used to kill that job and
rerun its trials; it now waits for the job to finish and collects its trials, with early stop
still applied while waiting. The job is identified by command line (`harbor` +
`band-<run_id>-`), so a reused pid is never adopted. Fake harbor runs as a real child
process (`fake_harbor.py`).

|---|---|---|
